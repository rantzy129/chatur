# DESIGN.md — Red Line VIP

## Visual World: Tactical Obsidian & Crimson HUD
A high-craft private syndicate interface engineered for competitive tactical integrity. No generic purple AI gradients, no repetitive three-card layouts, no meaningless kicker pills, and no low-contrast washed-out grays.

### Color Palette & Tokens
- **Background**: Deep Carbon Obsidian `#08090d`
- **Panels & Surfaces**: Tactical Graphite `#0e1117`, Elevated Card `#131722`
- **Hairline Borders**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, Active/Focus: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)`
- **Primary Brand Accent**: Red Line Crimson `#ef4444` / Deep Crimson `#dc2626`
- **Secondary Accent**: Cyber Gold `#f59e0b` (reserved exclusively for Lifetime tier)
- **Status Telemetry**: Precision Emerald `#10b981` (for operational status only)
- **High-Contrast Text**: Primary `#ffffff`, Secondary `#d4d4d8` (contrast ≥ 7:1)

### Typography
- **Headings**: Unforgiving, high-impact uppercase sans-serif with negative tracking (`tracking-tight` / `tracking-tighter`, `font-black`).
- **Telemetry & Technical Data**: Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, `ui-monospace`) with tabular numerals (`tabular-nums`) for memory offsets, license keys, prices, and timestamps.
- **Body Copy**: High-readability measure restricted to `60–72ch`.

### Impeccable Anti-Slop Enforcement
- ❌ **No Eyebrow/Kicker Pills**: Headings carry their own weight without decorative pill badges floating above them.
- ❌ **No Rainbow Gradient Text**: Emphasis is achieved through weight, scale, and monochromatic contrast.
- ❌ **No Repetitive Icon+Title+Card Triads**: Content is organized into functional technical matrices, asymmetric specs, and operational HUD instruments.
- ❌ **No Zero-Blur Colored Halos**: Depth is created with crisp 1px borders and soft, subtle shadows.
